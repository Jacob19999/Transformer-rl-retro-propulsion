## Project Overview

Research project validating explicit **convex (SOCP) powered-descent guidance** for thrust-vectoring control (TVC) in retro-propulsive landings on a physical **Electric Ducted Fan (EDF) drone** testbed. The goal is sim-to-real transfer from a custom Isaac Sim simulation to hardware.
