IMU calibration

applied on the host
Idle — choose a procedure below.
Gyro biasKeep the sensor completely still for 10 s.
Accelerometer · six positionsRest the sensor with each axis pointing up, then down, 3 s each. Solves offset and scale per axis.
MagnetometerStart, rotate slowly through every orientation, then Finish. Needs at least 50 % coverage.
Sensor gyro auto-zeroThe WTGAHRS1 clamps its gyro to zero when still, which hides the bias. Disable it before the gyro-bias step. This writes to the sensor's flash.
